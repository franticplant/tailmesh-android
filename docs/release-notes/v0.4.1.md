Tailmesh 0.4.1 fixes Proxy-Only Multi-Tailnet so your upstreams and listeners actually start, lets the phone reach WireGuard or SOCKS5 servers on its USB tether or hotspot, keeps local traffic out of the VPN on Android 13+, and adds a status-bar speed meter and a Logs screen.

Installs as an update over 0.4.0 and 0.3.x, keeping your tailnets and settings. Most phones want the `arm64-v8a` APK; use `universal` if unsure.

## New: speed meter in the status bar

**Settings → Speed in status bar** shows download over upload next to the clock, as the icon of an ongoing notification (Android 12+). It reads Android's own per-interface counters for Wi-Fi, mobile data and Ethernet rather than Tailmesh's accounting, so it means the same with the VPN on, off or Proxy-Only, and VPN traffic is not counted twice. It pauses while the screen is off and comes back after a reboot or an update.

## New: Logs screen

**Settings → Logs** gathers the app and engine logs (live), events and remote access in one screen. Search plain text or a regex, filter or highlight matches and step through them, narrow by source, follow the latest line, expand or copy a line, and share, save or copy what is shown.

## Fixes

- **Proxy-Only Multi-Tailnet now starts everything.** In 0.4.0 the tailnets came up, but WireGuard and SOCKS5 upstreams, routing rules and SOCKS5/inbound listeners did not until some setting was changed.
- **Inbound listeners come back after a restart.** They were set up a moment before their tailnet was running and never retried, so a forward such as LocalSend stayed down after a restart, an update or a mode switch. They now come back as soon as the tailnet is up, including after it recovers later.
- **Upstreams on the tether or hotspot work.** A WireGuard or SOCKS5 server on a device connected to the phone's USB tether or hotspot never connected, in VPN and Proxy-Only mode alike: the phone sent its packets out over Wi-Fi or mobile data instead. Private and link-local destinations are now routed by Android to the right interface.
- **Editing a listener in place takes effect.** Changing a listener's upstream without changing its port failed silently and kept the old one.
- **Listener traffic shows in Diagnostics.** Connections through SOCKS5 listeners now count towards their upstream's flows and bytes.
- **`@direct` listeners with hostnames.** A SOCKS5 client that sent a hostname through an `@direct` listener got "no such host": Go's resolver has no DNS server on Android. It now asks the engine's DNS server (your DoH resolver, or the network's).
- **Tailnets showing as not loaded after a background start.** The state poll started only when the Multi-Tailnet screen was opened, so after an update, reboot or always-on start the notification, Diagnostics and provisioning said every tailnet was not loaded. It now starts with the engine. Stopping Multi-Tailnet also hands the imported profiles back to standard mode again.
- **Live byte counts.** TCP flows through the VPN and both listener kinds only added their byte totals when the connection closed, so a long download showed 0 bytes in Diagnostics, and a connection that stayed open never counted. Bytes now count as they flow.

## Changes

- **Local traffic stays out of the VPN (Android 13+).** With broad capture and "LAN stays direct" on, private, link-local and multicast addresses are now excluded from the VPN itself instead of passing through Tailmesh, so local discovery, casting and printing keep working.
- **Proxy-Only Mode** now says in Settings that it covers Multi-Tailnet too, and remote control can switch it (`settings.setProxyOnly`).

## Under the hood

- The NDK is pinned and the Go build ID is cleared, so release builds reproduce on any machine.
